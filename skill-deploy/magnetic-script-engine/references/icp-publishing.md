# Publishing / education

- Typical documents: textbook chapters, course notes, ebooks, study guides and
  nonfiction excerpts.
- Build curiosity through the source's own question, contrast or conceptual structure.
- Do not simplify a claim into something the source does not support.
- Attribute contestable claims to the source or named author when the source does.
- Do not invent exam relevance, student behavior, consensus or examples.
- Watch especially for fragmented concepts and false certainty.
