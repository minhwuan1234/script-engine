# Corporate L&D / compliance / onboarding

- Typical documents: policies, SOPs, handbooks, product manuals and safety procedures.
- Make the source-stated action recognizable in a workplace situation without
  inventing a role, consequence or company practice.
- Preserve wording that defines duties, permissions, prohibitions and exceptions.
- Do not imply disciplinary, legal, safety or commercial consequences absent from the
  source.
- Do not mock employees, customers or job functions.
- Watch especially for transfer failure and overload.
