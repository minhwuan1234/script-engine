# Myth-buster

Use this shape only when the source or supplied audience brief explicitly identifies
or directly contradicts a belief. State that belief once, correct it with the exact
source-supported rule, and end on the correction. Never invent a common belief or say
that people usually misunderstand something.

When no belief is established, do not invent one or turn ordinary facts into a
question chain. Use a source-stated distinction or rule and its explicit boundary
if available; otherwise give a direct explanation and disclose the style limitation
in Engine notes. Do not claim a genuine myth has been corrected in that fallback.
