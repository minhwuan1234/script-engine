# Final review

Use these criteria to judge and improve the piece, without a prescribed review
sequence or mandatory written checklist. Factual, narrative and spoken quality
must be checked before a version is released; compare B with A before releasing B.
Review should lead to a revision when a weakness is found, not a silent declaration
that the draft has passed.

## Trace factual claims

For factual statements in titles, hooks, questions, voiceover and visuals, be able
to identify the support. A private ledger is optional; the relevant checks are:

- the exact supporting source passage
- the fact with every condition and qualifier attached
- where it appears in the version being reviewed; compare the two ledgers before
  releasing Version B
- whether its phrasing implies anything beyond the source

Delete any factual claim without an exact supporting passage. Evaluate production
choices and framing for implied claims; they do not need verbatim source wording. Do not print the ledger.

## Wrong-direction checks

1. **Source read-aloud:** each section received airtime but there is no clear
   communication objective or developing thought.
2. **Factual drift:** a number, threshold, condition or instruction was softened,
   rounded, completed or paraphrased into a different meaning.
3. **Everything included:** the script exceeds its budget or buries the chosen job.
4. **Same script twice:** both versions use the same beats and differ only in wording.
5. **Dead or generic hook:** the opening could introduce a different document,
   provides no useful reason to continue, or opens a question
   the script cannot answer from the source.
6. **Borrowed tension:** a hypothetical interpretation is presented as a common
   belief, or the source does not actually contradict it. Identify the exact rule
   behind the correction. Check that an illustrative scene adds no clinical claim,
   consequence or apparent real testimonial.

## Storytelling check

- Can the organizing idea be named without using the document title or style label?
  If the script is a paragraph-by-paragraph paraphrase, rebuild its beat order.
- Does each beat advance the opening's useful promise, rather than repeat a question
  or reveal a trivial label? Replace artificial suspense with a direct action or
  a source-supported decision, relationship or explanation.
- Read only the voiceover as continuous speech. Does it sound like someone helping
  the listener, or like someone explaining how the document was organized? Rework
  commentary about passages, references, “two places” and writing choices into the
  actual task or situation. Preserve useful references to documents the viewer uses.
- Check that beats connect as thoughts, without formulaic transitions, arbitrary
  “X does not replace Y” contrasts or a second telling of the same instruction.
- Do visuals show progression or explain a relationship where the source permits
  it? Repeated caption cards alone are insufficient when a useful demonstration is
  available. Do not add factual detail merely to create motion.

- Apply `references/tone-of-voice.md` to the spoken delivery: does a warm,
  knowledgeable speaker guide the listener through connected thoughts? Let sourced
  reasons explain actions and preserve their conditions. Replace staccato topic
  lists or forced jokes with useful conversation. Light wit can help; a joke is
  not a requirement. Keep critical instructions clear and preserve dignity.
- Apply the fixed High energy in `references/crazy-dial.md`: does the opening make
  a distinctive, honest promise, and does the script develop it? If the difference
  from a calm introduction is only a louder adjective, change the creative idea.
  Check that dramatic framing has not added an unsourced belief, stake or outcome.

- Practical test from the original healthcare skill: after watching, could the
  intended viewer apply the chosen action with its actual conditions, without a
  clinician or subject expert having to correct it? For an explanatory subject,
  could they explain the key distinction accurately?
- Narrative test: why does the viewer need each next line? If facts can be shuffled
  freely without affecting the experience, strengthen the throughline. A sourced
  reason, a meaningful decision or a demonstration can do this; invented stakes cannot.

## Each version on its own

Apply the required checks below to A before releasing A, and to B before releasing
B. Also check that the voice is speakable and consistent, information arrives in a
useful order, the opening promise is fulfilled, and visuals reinforce the narration.
A mechanism, misconception or emotional scene is never required when absent from
the source. Block or remove every unsupported action or threshold.

Repair the level at which a weakness occurs:

- Weak hook: improve the opening and the narrative beats needed to fulfill it.
- Stiff speech: rewrite the actual lines for the speaker and situation; preserve a
  consistent viewpoint and keep locked facts intact.
- Flat or assembled narrative: rethink the concept, information order or visual
  thread. Synonym changes and extra transitions cannot repair a weak idea.
- Factual drift: correct or remove the claim, then check any premise or payoff that
  depended on it.

Choose the repair that fits the issue; no fixed revision order or mandatory number
of rewrites applies. Check the affected piece again for factual meaning, the shared
communication objective and narrative effects before release. Describing a weakness
in Engine notes is not a substitute for repairing avoidable writing problems.
Keep the diagnosis and revision process private.

## The pair, before releasing B

- Both versions serve the same communication objective and legitimate takeaway, retaining the same
  locked facts and their conditions.
- Differences are meaningful in at least two of perspective, narrative architecture,
  sourced mechanism delivery, viewer experience and memory anchor, as defined in
  script craft. Judge the recognizable style experience, not compliance with a
  fixed beat template. Renaming or synonym substitution is insufficient.
- Each version is independently worth watching. A strong A does not compensate for
  a weak B, and the labels alone should not be needed to distinguish the experiences.
  Look for meaningful differences in how viewers discover and remember the material;
  different pronouns, tone or vocabulary do not establish a second concept.
- If the beat sequences are effectively identical, rebuild B's outline within its
  assigned style and recheck its quality. A has already been released and must not be
  rewritten. Do not sacrifice accuracy for contrast; disclose a source limitation
  in Engine notes when the two-dimension target cannot be met safely.

These are model self-review instructions, not proof of quality. Human evaluation
against source documents is still needed to assess fidelity and usefulness.

## Required checks

- Every factual statement traces to an exact source passage.
- Locked facts retain exact numbers, units, wording, conditions and exceptions.
- No two facts have been joined into an unsupported relationship.
- No title, question, transition or visual smuggles in a new premise.
- Red-flag rules for the chosen job remain present.
- Voiceover fits the app-supplied word budget.
- For healthcare, scope fits the time available without forcing a step count or
  recap. Q&A answers and Myth-buster corrections are supported by the source;
  unsourced interpretations and scenes are clearly hypothetical;
  a sparse source is never padded to meet a suggested count.
- The hook lasts at most 15 seconds at a natural spoken pace, has no separate
  word-count requirement, and passes the checks in
  `references/hook-quality.md`. The body begins at its actual end timestamp, advances
  its promise and pays it off without withholding a source-stated urgent action.
- Before releasing Version B, both versions contain the same locked facts but differ
  structurally.
- Each on-screen direction explains a source-supported action, sequence or contrast;
  remove decorative rows and any implied fact without a source passage.
- Each ending completes the thought. Where the job requires an action, its
  source-stated conditions and timing remain clear.
- Missing definitions, mechanisms and conflicts appear only in `Engine notes`.
- Factual assumptions: none.
