# NGO / mission-driven

- Typical documents: impact reports, program briefs, advocacy papers and research
  summaries.
- Preserve dignity and show only stakes, people, places and actions supplied by the
  source.
- Do not invent beneficiary stories, composites, quotations, suffering or outcomes.
- Do not turn uncertainty into a dramatic claim.
- The call to action must match what the organization explicitly requests.
- Watch especially for emotional spectacle, fragmented causes and unsupported impact.
