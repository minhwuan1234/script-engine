# Crazy Dial: High

Use the High setting for every new script. It changes the creative energy of both
versions, while their assigned narrative styles still determine their distinct
angles. There is no Low or Medium mode in this engine.

Make the difference visible in the opening thought, not by adding an adjective to
an ordinary introduction. Start with a source-grounded observation, a sharp
contrast, a moment already in motion, an honest challenge, or an unexpected visual
point of view. Choose what fits the source and the assigned style. The first line
should give the viewer a reason to follow the next one, and the script must pay off
that promise.

Let the writing have rhythm and character: a pointed line, a longer explanation
when the idea needs room, a quick turn, or a visual reveal. Use hard cuts, bold
type, an expressive object, or situational wit where they help the viewer understand.
High means a stronger creative choice, not constant speed, chopped sentences,
mandatory jokes, a cliffhanger at every timestamp, or a prescribed plot. Give
clinical instructions and sensitive moments the time and clarity they require.

The source sets the ceiling for dramatic stakes. Bring its real consequence or
decision into focus without enlarging it. A challenge to the viewer may be lively
without accusing them of a habit or belief the source never established. A
hypothetical thought must be clearly hypothetical and directly corrected by the
current source. Avoid clickbait, unsourced "most people" claims, invented cases,
testimonials, statistics, outcomes and medical mechanisms.

High never changes locked facts, numbers, thresholds, conditions, actions or
exceptions. Keep both versions' communication objective and factual basis the
same. `references/source-accuracy.md` governs any conflict between an energetic
line and a precise statement. `references/tone-of-voice.md` still governs warmth,
respect and speakable explanation.

Before releasing each version, compare its opening with a calm, literal way of
introducing the same source fact. If only one adjective or punctuation mark changed,
develop the concept, scene or visual thought further. Then read the whole voiceover:
the energy should carry an explanation or decision, not merely announce excitement.
