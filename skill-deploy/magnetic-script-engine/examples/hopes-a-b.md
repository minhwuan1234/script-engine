## Version B — First-person journey (caregiver): "Anything Else He's Taking?"

| Time | Voiceover | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:05 | Picking up Dad's new antibiotic. The pharmacist asks: anything else he's taking? | A pharmacy counter. A woman in her 50s holds a prescription bag. The pharmacist looks up. [Contextualize] |
| 0:05–0:10 | And I freeze. Blood pressure pills. Some vitamins. Something herbal? | Her thought bubble: blurry pill bottles that won't come into focus. |
| 0:10–0:15 | I'm not sure. And "not sure" is where medicine problems slip through. | The bubble pops. A small gap opens in a line of pills on screen. |

### Script
| Time | Voiceover | On screen |

| 0:39–0:47 | Or St. John's Wort, an herb sold right off the shelf, with some prescription antidepressants, may make depression worse. | A drugstore shelf. St. John's Wort meets a prescription bottle. |
| 0:47–0:56 | And Dad sees three doctors. Each one knows their own prescriptions. None of them sees the drugstore bag. | Three doctors, each holding one bottle. The drugstore bag sits outside the frame. [Build mental model] |
